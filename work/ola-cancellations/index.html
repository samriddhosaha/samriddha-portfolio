Ola ride cancellations

A Power BI report on one month of ride bookings, built to show why rides get cancelled.

Type
Personal project
Tools
SQL, Power BI
Data
Ola bookings, July 2024
Built
Jan 2025

01The question

Why do Ola rides get cancelled, and where could a team act to bring the cancellation rate down by 10%?

02The data

A dataset of Ola bookings for July 2024 (1 to 31 July). I cleaned and processed data from several sources before analysis.

03The method

SQL queries produced the figures, and a five-page Power BI report presents them: Overall, Vehicle Type, Revenue, Cancellation and Ratings.

04What I found

Of 103,024 bookings, 63,967 (62.1%) were completed. A further 28,933 were cancelled, a cancellation rate of 28.08%: 17.9% of bookings cancelled by the driver and 10.2% by the customer. Another 9.8% ended with no driver found.

  • Customers cancel mostly because of the driver. "Driver is not moving towards pickup" (30.2%) and "driver asked to cancel" (25.4%) together account for 55.7% of customer cancellations.
  • Drivers cancel mostly for their own reasons. Personal and car-related issues are 35.5% of driver cancellations; customer-related issues are 29.4%.

05What I'd do next

The 10% reduction is a target I set, not a forecast, and the project does not estimate how much a given change would move it. The next step is to size the opportunity from the dashboard's own numbers.

As arithmetic only: about 10,500 bookings were cancelled by customers, and 55.7% of those (about 5,850) cite driver behaviour at pickup. Halving that group would remove about 2,900 cancellations and move the rate from 28.1% to about 25.3%, a 10% relative drop. Whether such a change is achievable is a question for the operations team and the data cannot answer it.

I would also check how the cancellation definition treats the "driver not found" bookings, which sit outside the 28.08%.
